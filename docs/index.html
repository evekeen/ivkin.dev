About

I'm a startup founder and CTO at Enabled Health Inc, where we're building technology to help hospital patients stay strong and mobile—improving outcomes and helping hospitals turn beds faster.

As an indie hacker, I built and sold Ace Trace, a mobile sports tracking app, in summer 2025. Recently joined Winrate.com as a founding engineer. I've spent my career founding and building startups, exploring the intersection of AI and product development.

This is where I document the journey—the wins, the lessons, and everything in between.
